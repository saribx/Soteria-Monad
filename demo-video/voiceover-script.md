# Soteria demo film: voice-over script (45 s)

Matches the current timeline in `soteria-frontend/src/video/main.ts`: longer intro,
three app clips without zoom. Read at a calm, confident pace, about 2.5 to 3 words
per second. Leave the storm impact (13.3 to 15.0 s) and the final logo beat
without speech, so the sound design carries those moments.

## Recording notes

- Record dry (no music) at 48 kHz. Duck the music by about 8 dB under the voice and bring it back to full level for the storm and the end card.
- Each line starts slightly after the visual beat it describes, so the picture comes first and the voice confirms it.
- The film also works without a voice-over. Every claim in the script appears as on-screen text.
